## Global Macro
Heightened uncertainty dominated the narrative, centered on Trump administration Fed Chair nomination speculation (Kevin Warsh’s hawkish odds surged from ~25% to over 55% in betting markets) and renewed tariff threats that reinforced sticky inflation concerns. Risk assets faced pressure as cross-asset correlation with the Nasdaq rose sharply (~0.75–0.80), while gold attracted safe-haven bids (up ~2.5% in the period) and the DXY strengthened modestly.

Powell’s term-end dynamics added policy fog, with markets pricing in fewer rate cuts for 2026 than previously expected. Tariff rhetoric on key trading partners created ripple effects across equities and commodities, indirectly weighing on BTC’s risk-on correlation. Implications included a broader “risk-off tilt” that made BTC more sensitive to U.S. equity moves than in prior months, with gold-BTC decoupling emerging as a hedging signal for some portfolios.

Senate Agriculture Committee released the Digital Commodity Intermediaries Act draft, proposing CFTC oversight for spot crypto markets (with a scheduled hearing on Jan 27). Market reaction was cautiously positive but highlighted implementation uncertainties.

South Dakota House Bill 1155 was introduced, authorizing the state investment council to allocate up to 10% of public funds into Bitcoin with detailed custody and security requirements — a tangible state-level adoption signal.

The White House invited crypto and banking executives to a market-structure summit; the SEC simultaneously issued a statement clarifying rules for tokenized securities.

## Institutional Flows

Volatile rotation characterized the period: the early-January inflow surge gave way to mixed/redemption days mid-to-late. BlackRock’s IBIT and Fidelity’s FBTC remained the dominant players, yet flows were no longer unidirectional — several days saw net redemptions exceeding $200M, offset by sporadic large single-day inflows on positive regulatory headlines.

Institutional behavior: Institutions demonstrated “resilience via rotation” rather than outright exit, with some reallocating between spot ETFs and futures basis trades. On-chain ETF wallet activity showed profit-taking among newer entrants while long-term holders maintained positions.

## Social Layer & Sentiment

The Fear & Greed Index declined sharply, reaching ~24 by January 21 and flirting with extreme fear territory through the period’s end. Retail sentiment shifted noticeably from “institutional golden era” euphoria to “leverage watch + macro risks” caution, with daily X/Reddit volume on BTC topics increasing ~15–20% amid the consolidation.

Influencer/whale discussions & analyst opinions: Prominent whales and on-chain analysts emphasized ETF rotation mechanics, yet issued repeated warnings on over-leverage (funding rates, open interest) and rising supply churn. Analyst consensus became more nuanced — “healthy consolidation after the run” versus “watch the $86k–$88k support cluster closely.”
